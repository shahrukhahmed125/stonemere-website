About

Life management, defined

Trusted, practical support and coordination for the clients of care providers, solicitors and health professionals, and for older people, vulnerable adults and families facing difficulty or change.

Talk to Stonemere

Life
management

/ˈlaɪf ˈmænɪdʒmənt/  noun

One trusted point of contact who coordinates the practical aspects of a person’s life, and makes sure important matters are followed through.

Our services bridge the gap between traditional care, family support and everyday practical help. We manage appointments, correspondence, household matters and administration; liaise with healthcare and care providers; accompany clients when needed; coordinate moves, storage and property matters; and support people through hospital discharge, bereavement, the end of life and other crises.

What Stonemere is and is not

Not a care agency

We don’t provide personal care, nursing or medical treatment. We work alongside the people who do.

Not simply a concierge

We don’t just run errands. We take responsibility for matters, solve problems and see them through.

The keystone

The one person who holds the practical side together, so the family and every professional can rely on it.

How we work

Five principles behind every case

  1. One point of contact

    The client, the family and every professional know who is responsible.

  2. Alongside, never instead

    We complement care providers, health teams and solicitors and respect their roles.

  3. Followed through

    Matters are seen to completion, not passed on.

  4. The client’s wishes first

    We act with the client’s knowledge and consent, and keep their independence at the centre.

  5. Clear and written

    What we do, and what it costs, is agreed in writing before work begins.

Where we work

Rooted in Bath

Our primary service area is Bath and the surrounding area, initially concentrating on clients within about five miles of the city. We also serve the wider Bath & North East Somerset area, and can coordinate matters further afield when an existing client or their family needs help.
