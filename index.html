Who we are

When a client needs more than your service covers, someone has to pick up the rest. That is what we do.

Stonemere Life Management provides professional support to businesses and organisations whose clients, residents or families need practical coordination beyond their core service.

We manage appointments, family liaison, property matters, welfare visits and complex day-to-day issues, giving you one reliable point of contact. We are not a care agency, and we are not simply a concierge.

What life management means

Life management

How an arch holds

  1. Everyone holds a piece. The family, the carers, the GP, the solicitor, the property and the trades each carry part of a client’s life.
  2. Without a keystone, nothing holds. Matters fall between organisations, messages go unanswered, and nobody owns the whole picture.
  3. One point of contact. Everything holds. Stonemere sits at the top of the arch, coordinating every party and following each matter through.

How a referral works

Four plain steps, no paperwork to start

Your first message doesn’t need the client’s full name or medical details. We take those safely once we have spoken.

Build a referral
  1. 01

    Message or call

    WhatsApp us or call 01225 338436. Tell us who you are and, in outline, what the client needs.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope in writing

    We talk it through, confirm what we will take on and how it will be paid for, and put it in writing before anything starts.

  3. 03

    One named contact takes it on

    The same person coordinates every matter, liaises with everyone involved and follows each one through.

  4. 04

    You stay informed

    We keep you and the family updated, with the client’s consent, so you always know where things stand.

What we do and what we don't

We do

Coordinate, liaise, attend, organise, arrange and follow through.

Practical coordination, communication, representation at appointments, property and possessions, logistics and problem-solving, carried through to completion.

We never

Replace the professionals already involved.

We do not provide medical treatment, nursing, regulated personal care, legal advice or financial advice. When those are needed, we make sure the right people are involved and we work alongside them.

The standards behind every case

  • Enhanced DBS-checkedto the standard expected for work with vulnerable adults
  • Confidentialinformation shared only with consent or where the law requires it
  • Clear boundariesagreed in writing, alongside other professionals, never instead of them
  • One named contactfor the client, the family and every referrer
