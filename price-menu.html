Clear charges, agreed upfront

Ongoing support runs on a simple membership credited to the client’s own account. Everything else is explained and agreed in advance. Current rates are confirmed in writing.

The client account

How the membership works

  1. 1

    Choose a level

    Silver, Gold or Platinum, depending on how much support is likely to be needed.

  2. 2

    Payments credit the account

    Monthly membership payments go into the client’s own Stonemere account.

  3. 3

    Funds are used as we work

    The account is drawn down against Stonemere’s time and services, and you can see how.

Membership levels

Three levels

Silver

Occasional, dependable help

  • A named Stonemere contact
  • Help with appointments, admin and the odd practical problem
  • Typically suits clients who are largely independent

Regular, ongoing coordination

  • Regular practical help across the organisations involved
  • Typically suits several organisations being involved
