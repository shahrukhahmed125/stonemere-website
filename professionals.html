For professionals

Hand over the practical side. We follow it through.

For care providers, solicitors, healthcare and property professionals whose clients need practical coordination beyond your core service. One named contact, a clear remit and no chasing.

Build a referralCall 01225 338436

The remit

Where your service ends, ours begins

Every service has edges. The practical matters just outside them, such as the empty house, the family abroad, the appointment nobody can attend or the clearance after a death, are where clients come to harm and professionals lose time.

Stonemere takes those matters on, coordinates everyone involved and sees each one through, working alongside you and never instead of you.

How we work with each profession

Domiciliary care companies

Your carers are often first to notice what is going wrong outside the care plan. We pick it up so your team can stay on care.

You hand over

  • Post, bills and household admin that need attention
  • Contact with family who are hard to reach or far away
  • Appointments the client needs accompanying to
  • Repairs and contractors at the client’s home

You get back

  • One named contact for the client’s practical matters
  • Clear boundaries: we never deliver personal care
  • Updates you agree with us, with the client’s consent

Solicitors, executors and deputies

Probate, deputyship and attorney matters stall on practical tasks. We do the legwork, in line with your instructions.

You hand over

  • Vacant property checks and property oversight
  • Contractor coordination and access
  • Possessions, storage, clearance and disposal
  • Attendance with the client at solicitor and other appointments

You get back

  • Matters followed through, not passed on
  • A single point of contact for the family
  • Decisions referred back to you, not made for you

Healthcare professionals

Discharge teams, GPs, community clinicians and social prescribers: going home safely depends on more than the care package.

You hand over

  • Preparing the home before discharge
  • Transport, shopping and essential supplies
  • Coordination with carers, family and services
  • Accompaniment to follow-up appointments

You get back

  • The practical side handled, so plans hold
  • A local contact who knows the client
  • Work that sits alongside the clinical plan, never inside it

Property professionals

Estate and letting agents and others managing a property linked to an older or vulnerable client.

You hand over

  • Access, keys and contractor visits
  • Checks on empty or changing properties
  • Downsizing, clearance and disposal
  • Liaison with the family and the client’s representatives

You get back

  • A dependable contact on the ground in Bath
  • Matters coordinated with everyone involved
  • Problems flagged early
