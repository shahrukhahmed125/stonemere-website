Service area 03

Property & Asset Management

Homes and possessions looked after, prepared and, when the time comes, cleared or sold, with executors, attorneys and families kept informed.

Ask about this areaCall 01225 338436

What this covers

7 services, one contact

Each one can be arranged on its own or as part of ongoing support.

  1. 01

    Property oversight

    Someone dependable keeping an eye on a client’s property and dealing with what comes up.

  2. 02

    Vacant property checks

    Regular visits to an empty property, at a frequency agreed with you, with anything of concern reported and acted on.

  3. 03

    Contractor coordination

    Trades and contractors found, given access, supervised and checked.

  4. 04

    Downsizing

    A move to a smaller home planned and coordinated at the client’s pace.

  5. 05

    House clearance

    Full or partial clearances handled sensitively, with items kept, distributed or disposed of as instructed.

  6. 06

    Storage and possessions management

    Belongings catalogued, stored and looked after until decisions are made.

  7. 07

    Property or asset disposal

    Coordination of the practical steps when a property, vehicle or other asset is sold or disposed of.
