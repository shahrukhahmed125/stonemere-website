Services

Four service areas, one trusted contact

Everything Stonemere does falls into four areas. Each can be arranged on its own, and all of them are coordinated by the same named person.

Ask which fitsPrice menu

The four service areas

  1. 01 Family & Client Support Family liaison, welfare visits, appointments, admin, end-of-life and post-death coordination
  2. 02 Care & Professional Liaison Case management, health and care coordination, care-provider liaison and appointments
  3. 03 Property & Asset Management Oversight, vacant property checks, contractors, downsizing, clearance, storage and disposal
  4. 04 Urgent & Complex Case Support Same-day and out-of-hours help, crisis coordination and complex multi-party cases

Choosing

Not sure where to start?

Most cases touch more than one area. Tell us the situation and we will shape the support around it, with one person responsible for all of it.

  • A client struggling at home, family far awayFamily & Client Support
  • Several services and nobody joining them upCare & Professional Liaison
  • An empty or changing propertyProperty & Asset Management
  • Something has happened todayUrgent & Complex Cases
