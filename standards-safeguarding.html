Enhanced DBS checks

Everyone who works with Stonemere clients holds an Enhanced DBS check, the level of check appropriate for work with vulnerable adults. We will never send anyone into a client's home who has not been vetted to this standard.

Safeguarding adults

We follow a written safeguarding policy based on the six safeguarding principles set out in the Care Act 2014:

Empowerment

Supporting people to make their own decisions.

Prevention

Acting early, before harm occurs.

Proportionality

The least intrusive response appropriate to the risk.

Protection

Support for those in greatest need.

Partnership

Working with communities and professionals.

Accountability

Being transparent and accountable for what we do.

If we're worried that someone is at risk of abuse or neglect, we act. We follow our policy, involve the person wherever possible, and refer to Bath & North East Somerset Council's adult safeguarding team where appropriate.

Respecting choice and capacity

We work in line with the Mental Capacity Act 2005. We assume people can make their own decisions unless it's established otherwise; we support them to decide; and we respect their right to make choices others might not. Where someone has a Lasting Power of Attorney or a court-appointed deputy, we work with them within the scope of their authority.

Confidentiality and data protection

We handle personal information in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We collect only what we need, store it securely, and share it only with consent or where the law requires. Families receive the information the person we support has agreed to share.

Read our privacy notice for full details.

Clear professional boundaries

We always

  • Work within our remit
  • Involve the right professionals
  • Record what we do
  • Tell you honestly when something is outside our expertise

We never

  • Provide medical treatment or nursing
  • Provide regulated personal care
  • Give legal advice
  • Give financial advice

When medical, nursing, personal care, legal or financial expertise is needed, we make sure the appropriate specialist organisations and professionals are involved, and we work alongside them.

Handling money and purchases

Nothing is bought on a client's behalf without approval. Purchases are recorded, and your client account shows how membership funds have been used. Procurement costs, transport, third-party charges and Crisis Management are always explained and agreed in advance.

Complaints and feedback

If something isn't right, we want to know. You can raise a concern by phone, by WhatsApp or in writing, and we will acknowledge it promptly, look into it properly and respond fully. Feedback of every kind helps us improve.

Raising a concern about someone

In an emergency: if someone is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent medical advice, call NHS 111. If you're worried that an adult is being abused or neglected, contact Bath & North East Somerset Council's adult safeguarding team.

You can also tell us about a concern directly, and we will make sure it reaches the right people.
